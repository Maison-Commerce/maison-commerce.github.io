# Product Page Brief: Dreaver — Digital Gift Card

## Project Overview

* **Brand:** Dreaver (dreaver.nl) — #1 treadmill specialist in the Netherlands; compact, quiet walking treadmills for the home.
* **Page Type:** Product (PDP) — digital gift card
* **Primary Goal:** Convert the *gift-buyer* to purchase a digital gift card (€10 / €25 / €50 / €100 / €200), with a secondary goal of low-cost new-customer acquisition via redemption.
* **Target Audience:** The giver, not the walker — partners/family who know someone wants to move more but don't want to pick the wrong model; "can't-go-wrong" gifters buying for parents/colleagues; and last-minute buyers who need instant delivery. Reassurance, ease, thoughtfulness and speed drive them.

---

## PAGE STRUCTURE & ELEMENTS

### 1. Hero Section

**Purpose:** In one screen, communicate *what* this is (a digital Dreaver gift card), *why* it's a great gift (the gift of movement, they choose), and let the buyer act immediately (pick a value, add to cart). Kill the two biggest hesitations up front: "is this a safe gift?" and "will it arrive in time?"

**Layout:**
* Two-column on desktop (≈55/45). Left: headline, subhead, denomination selector, CTA, micro-trust row. Right: hero visual.
* Denomination chips sit *above the fold*, directly under the subhead — the selector is the hero, not an afterthought.
* Mobile: single column, stacked in order — visual (compact) → headline → subhead → denomination chips (2-per-row grid, large tap targets) → CTA → micro-trust row. CTA becomes sticky on scroll.
* Generous whitespace around the card mock-up; airy, premium, uncluttered.

**Copy Required:**
* **Headline:** Lead with the gift-of-movement angle. Primary: **"Geef het cadeau van beweging."** Alt to test: *"Geef beweging cadeau. Zij kiezen hoe."*
* **Subheadline:** *"Een digitale Dreaver-cadeaubon — direct per e-mail, in te wisselen op de hele collectie, zonder vervaldatum."*
* **Body Copy:** None in hero beyond subhead — keep it clean.
* **CTA Button:** **"Verstuur dit cadeau"** → adds selected gift-card variant to cart. Secondary text link below: *"Hoe werkt het?"* (anchor-scrolls to section 4).
* **Micro-trust row (under CTA, icon + 2–3 words each):** ⚡ Direct per e-mail · 🎁 Kies zelf een verzenddatum · ♾️ Geen vervaldatum · ↩️ 30 dagen bedenktijd

**Visual Direction:**
* **Photography/Imagery:** A beautifully designed, Dreaver-branded digital gift card mock-up (floating, subtle shadow) paired with — or layered over — a warm, natural lifestyle moment: someone walking on a Dreaver at home, smiling, relaxed clothing, daylight. Gifting warmth, not "voucher." Avoid ribbon/bow clichés and cold gift-card stock art.
* **Color Palette:** Warm off-white/cream background (brand base), dark charcoal text, one Dreaver accent for the card and CTA. *(Confirm exact hex against Dreaver brand guide.)*
* **Visual References:** Apple-gift-card cleanliness meets Dreaver's warm, human lifestyle photography. Soft, real, aspirational-but-attainable.

**Design Notes:**
* Selected denomination chip must have an unmistakable active state (filled accent + checkmark). Price updates the CTA context if helpful.
* Consider a subtle entrance animation on the card mock-up (gentle float/tilt) — restrained, not gimmicky.
* Sticky mobile CTA carries the currently-selected value.

### 2. USP / Trust Bar

**Purpose:** Immediately reinforce the three things a gift-buyer cares about most, reframed from Dreaver's product USPs to *gift* USPs.

**Layout:**
* Full-width horizontal bar directly under hero. Three equal columns, icon-left or icon-top.
* Mobile: stack to three rows, or a swipeable single row. Keep it tight — one line each.

**Copy Required:**
* **Body Copy (3 USPs):**
  1. **Direct geleverd** — in de inbox binnen enkele minuten.
  2. **Zij kiezen zelf** — inwisselbaar op elke Dreaver.
  3. **Altijd goed** — geen maat, geen kleur, geen gedoe.
* **CTA Button:** None.

**Visual Direction:**
* **Imagery:** Simple, consistent line icons (delivery/lightning, choice/hand, checkmark). Match Dreaver's existing icon style.
* **Color Palette:** Neutral background (cream or white), charcoal text, accent icons.

**Design Notes:**
* Keep visually quiet — this is a reassurance strip, not a feature section. Subtle divider or background tint to separate from hero.

### 3. Why a Dreaver Gift Card (The Gift of Movement)

**Purpose:** The emotional core. Sell the *idea* that a Dreaver gift card is a thoughtful, safe, on-brand gift — movement that fits the recipient's life, on their terms — before getting into mechanics.

**Layout:**
* Section headline + short intro line, then 3–4 benefit cards in a row (desktop) / stacked or 2×2 (mobile).
* Alternating or single supporting lifestyle image optional. Airy spacing.

**Copy Required:**
* **Headline:** *"Het mooiste cadeau? Beweging die past in hún leven."*
* **Body Copy (benefit cards, headline + one line each):**
  1. **Bewegen zonder druk** — geen fitnessregime, maar kleine stappen die passen in de dag.
  2. **Zij kiezen hun Dreaver** — het model, het moment en het tempo dat bij hen past.
  3. **Een cadeau met bewezen liefde** — 75.000+ mensen bewegen al met Dreaver.
  4. **Perfect, ook op het laatste moment** — direct geleverd, altijd raak.
* **CTA Button:** Optional soft link — *"Bekijk de collectie"* (opens range in new tab) — keep secondary so it doesn't pull buyers off the page.

**Visual Direction:**
* **Photography/Imagery:** Warm, candid lifestyle — someone walking while working/watching TV, a parent moving at home, natural light. Reinforce "fits the life you already have." Faces welcome; real, unposed feel.
* **Color Palette:** Cream/white cards on soft background; accent used sparingly for icons or a highlighted card.

**Design Notes:**
* Tone must never guilt ("get fit") — this is care and possibility. Copy team: keep it warm and permission-giving.
* Cards can carry small brand icons rather than photos to keep the section calm.

### 4. How It Works (3 Steps)

**Purpose:** Remove the single biggest friction of any *digital* gift card — "what actually happens after I pay?" Spell out delivery, timing, and personalization so the buyer trusts the mechanics.

**Layout:**
* Three numbered steps, horizontal on desktop (connected by a light line/arrow), vertical stack on mobile.
* Each step: number badge + icon + short heading + one line.

**Copy Required:**
* **Headline:** *"Zo werkt het — in twee minuten geregeld."*
* **Body Copy (steps):**
  1. **Kies een bedrag** — €10 tot €200.
  2. **Personaliseer** — voeg een persoonlijke boodschap toe en kies wanneer de bon wordt verstuurd.
  3. **Direct in de inbox** — de ontvanger krijgt de cadeaubon per e-mail met een unieke code. Liever tastbaar? Je kunt 'm ook printen.
* **Supporting line under steps:** *"Verstuur nu of plan het op de dag zelf. De ontvanger wisselt de bon in op de hele Dreaver-collectie."*
* **CTA Button:** **"Kies een bedrag"** → scrolls back to hero selector / repeats chips inline.

**Visual Direction:**
* **Imagery:** Clean 3-step iconography or a simple illustrated sequence (amount → message/calendar → inbox). Optional small screenshot of the branded e-card as delivered.
* **Color Palette:** White/cream, accent number badges.

**Design Notes:**
* Explicitly state: *who* it can be sent to (buyer's own inbox to forward, or straight to recipient), *when* (now or scheduled date), and that it's **printable**. These three facts resolve most gift-buyer doubt.
* If scheduled-send / personal message require an app not yet installed, flag to dev (see Functional Notes at end).

### 5. What They Can Spend It On + "Chip In Together"

**Purpose:** Reassure the buyer the card is genuinely useful across the range, and honestly handle the value gap (treadmills start ~€297; cards top out at €200) by turning it into an angle: combine cards / contribute together.

**Layout:**
* Two-part section. Top: a light "redeemable on everything" strip showing product categories (Loopbanden · Accessoires · Recovery). Bottom: a warm "chip in together" callout box.
* Desktop: category thumbnails in a row; callout as a full-width tinted band beneath. Mobile: stacked.

**Copy Required:**
* **Headline:** *"In te wisselen op alles van Dreaver."*
* **Body Copy:** *"Van loopbanden (vanaf €297) tot accessoires en recovery — de ontvanger kiest zelf waar de bon aan wordt besteed."*
* **"Chip in together" callout — Heading:** *"Samen een groter cadeau geven?"*
* **Callout body:** *"Meerdere cadeaubonnen kunnen gecombineerd worden. Geef samen met familie, vrienden of collega's — en help iemand aan hun eigen Dreaver."*
* **CTA Button:** None (or soft *"Bekijk wat er mogelijk is"*).

**Visual Direction:**
* **Photography/Imagery:** Small, clean product category shots (treadmill, accessory, recovery/massage) on neutral backgrounds — consistent lighting. Callout box uses a warm accent tint to feel inviting.
* **Color Palette:** Category strip neutral; callout in soft accent/cream to distinguish it.

**Design Notes:**
* **Do not imply** "gift a treadmill for €50." Framing is "a great start toward" / "combine." Honesty here protects brand trust and lifts average gift value.
* Only promise gift-card stacking if Shopify/theme supports it — confirm before this copy ships (see Functional Notes).

### 6. Social Proof & Guarantees

**Purpose:** Borrow Dreaver's strongest existing trust assets to reassure a buyer who may not know the brand, and de-risk the purchase.

**Layout:**
* Two bands. Top: social proof (Trustpilot score + user count + 2–3 short testimonials with faces/first names). Bottom: three guarantee badges in a row.
* Mobile: testimonials become a swipeable carousel; badges stack to a 1×3 or 3×1 row.

**Copy Required:**
* **Headline:** *"75.000+ mensen bewegen al met Dreaver."*
* **Body Copy:** Trust line — *"4,6/5 op Trustpilot — 3.350+ beoordelingen."* Pull 2–3 testimonials, favouring gift-relevant ones if available (e.g. *"Gekregen van mijn dochter…"*).
* **Guarantee badges:**
  * ♾️ **Geen vervaldatum** — de bon verloopt niet. *(Confirm policy.)*
  * 🔒 **Veilig betalen** — iDEAL, Klarna, PayPal & meer.
  * ↩️ **30 dagen bedenktijd** — op elke bestelling met de bon.
* **CTA Button:** None.

**Visual Direction:**
* **Photography/Imagery:** Real customer photos + first names, matching the style used on Dreaver's existing PDPs. Trustpilot green star visual. Simple badge icons.
* **Color Palette:** Neutral background; Trustpilot green as the only "foreign" color; brand accent on badges.

**Design Notes:**
* Reuse existing review assets/components for consistency and speed. Don't invent proof.

### 7. FAQ + Final CTA

**Purpose:** Clear remaining doubts (delivery, expiry, usage, refunds) and give a final, low-anxiety push to buy.

**Layout:**
* Accordion FAQ (collapsed by default), then a closing CTA band repeating the denomination selector.
* Mobile: full-width accordion; sticky CTA remains available.

**Copy Required:**
* **FAQ Headline:** *"Veelgestelde vragen"*
* **Questions (answers on-page):**
  1. Hoe wordt de cadeaubon geleverd? — *Digitaal, per e-mail, met unieke code — meestal binnen enkele minuten.*
  2. Kan ik een verzenddatum kiezen? *(Confirm)* — *Ja, verstuur nu of plan op de dag zelf.*
  3. Kan ik een persoonlijk bericht toevoegen? *(Confirm)* — *Ja.*
  4. Waar kan de bon voor gebruikt worden? — *De hele collectie: loopbanden, accessoires en recovery.*
  5. Wat als het bedrag niet genoeg is voor een loopband? — *Combineer met een eigen bijdrage of andere cadeaubonnen.*
  6. Heeft de bon een vervaldatum? *(Confirm)*
  7. Kan de ontvanger het restbedrag bewaren? *(Confirm partial redemption)*
  8. Kan ik de bon printen? — *Ja.*
  9. Kan ik de bon inruilen voor geld? — *Nee.*
* **Final CTA Headline:** *"Klaar om beweging cadeau te geven?"*
* **Reassurance line:** *"Twijfel je over het bedrag? Je kunt niet misgokken — zij kiezen zelf."*
* **CTA Button:** **"Verstuur dit cadeau"** → add to cart.

**Visual Direction:**
* **Imagery:** Minimal — accordion is text. Final CTA band may reuse the hero card mock-up on a warm accent background.
* **Color Palette:** Accent band for the closing CTA to end on brand warmth.

**Design Notes:**
* Keep FAQ answers short and reassuring. The closing band should feel calm and confident, not hard-sell.

## OVERALL DESIGN GUIDELINES

**Visual Style:** Modern, warm-minimal, premium-but-approachable. Clean and airy with human warmth — wellness lifestyle, not clinical fitness. Should feel like a natural, elevated extension of Dreaver's existing site.

**Color Approach:**
* **Primary:** Warm off-white / cream — dominant background, keeps the page light and calm. *(Confirm exact brand value.)*
* **Secondary:** Dark charcoal / near-black — headlines and body text; strong, legible contrast.
* **Accent:** Dreaver's signature brand accent — reserved for CTAs, the selected denomination chip, badge icons, and the "chip in together" / final CTA bands. Use sparingly for maximum pull. *(Confirm exact hex against brand guide; Trustpilot green is the only permitted non-brand color, and only in the review component.)*

**Photography Direction:**
* **Style:** Lifestyle-led, with clean product/studio shots only for the "what they can spend it on" category strip and the gift-card mock-up.
* **Mood:** Bright, natural, warm daylight. Optimistic and calm — never high-intensity gym energy.
* **Subjects:** Real people moving at home in everyday settings (working while walking, watching TV, a parent mid-day); the branded digital gift card itself; product categories.
* **Composition notes:** Airy negative space, subjects off-center, natural candid framing. Consistent lighting across all product shots. Diverse, relatable, everyday people — not models mid-workout.

**Layout Principles:**
* **Spacing:** Airy and generous — let sections breathe; whitespace signals premium and reduces buying anxiety.
* **Grid:** Mostly strict, centered content column with comfortable max-width; occasional two-column hero/callout for rhythm. Avoid busy asymmetry.
* **Visual hierarchy priorities:** (1) denomination selector + CTA, (2) the gift-of-movement message, (3) how-it-works clarity, (4) trust/proof. The buyer should always be one glance from "pick a value and send."

## COPY TONE & VOICE

* **Brand voice:** Warm, relatable, encouraging, human. Short sentences. Anti-perfectionism — celebrates small, sustainable steps. Reassuring and permission-giving toward the gift-buyer.
* **Key phrases / language to use:** "beweging die past in het leven dat je al hebt," "geen gedoe," "lekker bewegen," "kleine stappen," "zij kiezen zelf," "direct per e-mail," "altijd raak." Frame the gift as *care and possibility*.
* **Words/phrases to avoid:** Guilt or pressure framing ("je moet sporten," "word eindelijk fit"), rigid fitness/performance language, "voucher"-style coldness, and anything implying a low-tier card buys a full treadmill. No hard-sell urgency that clashes with the warm tone.
* **Language:** Dutch for the primary site (dreaver.nl); mirror all copy to English for the /en version.

## FUNCTIONAL / DEV NOTES (for scoping — outside the design brief proper)

* **Platform:** Shopify native gift card product recommended (unique codes, balance tracking, partial redemption, store-wide use out of the box). Confirm theme's gift-card template vs. custom page.
* **Confirm before ship:** scheduled-send + personal-message support (may need a gifting app); gift-card **stacking** at checkout (required for the "chip in together" claim); expiry policy + NL consumer-law check; partial-redemption behaviour; printable e-card PDF.
* **Denominations:** five fixed variants — €10 / €25 / €50 / €100 / €200. No custom amount. Recommend visually highlighting **€100** as "most chosen" anchor (validate with data once live).
* **Analytics:** tag page + gift-card SKU to measure redemption rate and new-customer acquisition from redeemers.
