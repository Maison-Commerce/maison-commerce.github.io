The ritual

Three steps. Under a minute.

No barista, no ceremony anxiety.

1
Scoop.

One curve of the bamboo scoop into your cup.

2
Whisk with a little hot water until frothy.

Twenty seconds of W-shaped strokes. That's the whole technique.

3
Pour over ice or milk into your café cup, and go.

Lid on, out the door. The cup was in the box.

Craft & provenance
