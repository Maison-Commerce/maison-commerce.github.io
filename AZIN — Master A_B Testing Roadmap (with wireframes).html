M1PDP

Mix colors and keep the two-pack discount

HYP-PDP-00★ Client priority

Let shoppers build a two-pack from two different colours and still get the bundle price — instead of forcing two of the same colour.

Primary: Two-pack bundle ATCLever: Bundling / offer clarityEffort: HighPriority: 9.9/10

Problem

The 2-pack is a single SKU ("2 Boeketten") of one colour. Wanting two colours means losing the offer or guessing whether the cart will still discount.

Solution

Bundle builder: choose colour 1 + colour 2, live summary, original vs bundle price + saving, one "Voeg 2-pack toe" CTA that adds two single SKUs at the approved price.

Hypothesis

If AZIN lets customers select two different colors while automatically preserving the two-pack discount, then two-pack bundle add-to-cart rate, units per order, and purchase CVR will increase, because shoppers can receive both variety and the advertised multi-buy value without discount-code uncertainty.

M2Cart drawer

Automatically apply the sitewide discount

HYP-CART-01⚠ After QA

Show the promised ZOMER15 saving inside the drawer automatically, so shoppers see the final price without remembering or entering a code.

Primary: Cart-to-checkout rateLever: Price transparencyEffort: MediumPriority: 9.8/10 (post-QA)

Problem

The banner promotes 15% off with ZOMER15, but the drawer shows the original €79,95 — no discount state, saving, or final total. The advertised saving is invisible at the cart stage.

Solution

Auto-apply the active sitewide discount on eligible items: original subtotal, a clear discount line, updated subtotal, and "continues into checkout" — no code entry, using Shopify's real discount logic.

Hypothesis

If AZIN automatically applies and displays the active sitewide discount in the cart drawer, then cart-to-checkout and purchase rates will increase, because shoppers will see the promised saving without remembering or entering a code.

M3PDP

Minimal size information above the fold

HYP-PDP-01

Surface the two facts shoppers most need — height and stem count — in one compact line above the CTA, instead of only in the description.

Primary: PDP ATC rateLever: Product clarityEffort: LowPriority: 9.7/10

Problem

<20% of visitors scroll to the sizing info. The ~60 cm length and 20-stem count sit in the lower description, so most decide without them.

Solution

One verified line above the CTA: "± 60 cm hoog · 20 stelen". No vase dims or unmaintained attributes; kept visually secondary to price.

Hypothesis

If AZIN shows verified height and stem count in one compact line above the CTA, then PDP add-to-cart rate will increase, because visitors can validate basic scale and quantity without scrolling or adding visual clutter.

Surface Summer Collection, Bestsellers and categories without a "Shop" expansion tap; drop the redundant Home link and duplicates.

M8Collection

HYP-COL-03Needs auditable rules

Help uncertain shoppers spot the socially-validated options in a mixed grid with accurate, auditable labels.

Primary: Labeled-card CTRLever: Social proof / merchandisingEffort: LowPriority: 8.3/10

Problem

Solution

Execution

Recommended order

Phase 0 · QA (unblocks gated tests)

Fix before building

Standardize the free-shipping threshold and prove drawer/checkout totals match (unblocks M2); define auditable label rules (unblocks M8); standardize height/stem-count & filter attributes (supports M3, M9); confirm the mix-and-match discount rule, eligible families & stacking (supports M1).

Phase 1 · Cheap PDP clarity

M3 · M4

Low-effort wins on the biggest leak (85.7% PDP→cart). Run separately — both add content above the CTA.

Phase 2 · Commercial levers

M1 · M2

Mix-colors two-pack (your priority) and auto-discount (after Phase 0 QA). Judge both on contribution margin.

Phase 3 · Navigation & trust

M5 · M7 · M6

Homepage collection strip and mobile menu, plus Trustpilot on collection. Keep M5 and M7 separate to preserve attribution.

Phase 4 · Data-gated merchandising

M8 · M9

Labels and filters, once the underlying product data is clean and auditable.

Measurement: use unique sessions per surface as the denominator; treat clicks/impressions as diagnostic and decide on purchase CVR, revenue, and contribution margin per eligible session. Report mobile vs desktop and new vs returning separately. Shopify is the source of truth for orders/revenue; GA4 for directional journey analysis only. Wireframes are schematic — spacing, imagery and exact copy are finalized in design; illustrative figures (ratings, discount amounts, review counts, delivery dates) must be validated against real data before build.
